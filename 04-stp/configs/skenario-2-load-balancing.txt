! Skenario 2: root berbeda per VLAN (load balancing)
! Jalankan SETELAH konfigurasi skenario 1. VLAN 2 harus sudah ada di semua switch.

! --- CORE1: root VLAN 1, cadangan VLAN 2 ---
configure terminal
spanning-tree vlan 1 priority 0
spanning-tree vlan 2 priority 4096
end
write memory

! --- CORE2: root VLAN 2, cadangan VLAN 1 ---
configure terminal
spanning-tree vlan 2 priority 0
spanning-tree vlan 1 priority 4096
end
write memory

! Verifikasi di semua switch
! show spanning-tree vlan 1
! show spanning-tree vlan 2
